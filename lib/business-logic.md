## How the Process Works

1. **Hanging:** boards are cut and screwed to framing.
2. **Taping:** joint tape and first coat of compound are applied.
3. **Mudding:** additional coats are applied and feathered out, with drying time between coats.
4. **Sanding:** surfaces are sanded smooth. Expect fine dust.
5. **Ready for primer and paint.**
